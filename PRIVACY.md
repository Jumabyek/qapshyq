# Qapshyq Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Qapshyq is an offline archive manager. Your privacy is simple to describe:

- **No data collection.** Qapshyq does not collect, store or send any personal information.
- **No internet connection.** The app does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry.
- **Your files stay on your computer.** Archives are opened, extracted and created only on your device, in the folders you choose.
- **Passwords** you type for protected archives are used only in memory while the app is running. They are never saved to disk or sent anywhere.
- **Settings** (light/dark theme, window size, last opened folder) are stored locally on your computer by the operating system and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

If you contact us through GitHub (for example by opening an issue), GitHub's own privacy policy applies to the information you choose to share there.
